  • ☐ Arabian Desert (2.3 million km²)
  • ☐ Gobi Desert (1.3 million km²)
  • ☐ Kalahari (0.9 million km²)
3. The map shows Antarctica. What atmospheric phenomenon was discovered here in 1985?
  • ☐ Ozone hole
  • ☐ Polar vortex
  • ☐ Aurora Australis
  • ☐ Magnetic reversal

Answer key

  1. Gulf Stream — The Gulf Stream carries warm water from the Caribbean across the Atlantic.
  2. Sahara (9.2 million km²) — The Sahara is roughly the size of the entire United States.
  3. Ozone hole — The ozone hole led to the Montreal Protocol (1987), one of the most successful environmental treaties.
